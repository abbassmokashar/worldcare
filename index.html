Ask Our Team
Who do you supply?

We supply pharmacies, hospitals, clinics and other healthcare providers, as well as wholesale and bulk buyers, across Nigeria.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We distribute to all 36 states, operating from our head office in Lagos and branches in Kano and Port Harcourt.

What kinds of products do you carry?

Our focus is ophthalmic (eye care) medicines, alongside anti-inflammatory and anti-infective products, with antimalarial and other therapeutic areas coming as we expand.

How do I place an order or request a quote?

Call us on +234 706 666 4492, message us on WhatsApp, or email info@worldcarepharma.com and our team will respond.

How do you maintain product quality?

We import registered medicines, follow regulatory requirements, and use safe storage and cold-chain practices to protect product integrity.
